Getting Started

1

Register Your Business

Go to /register/ and select your business category (Restaurant, Retail, Pharmacy, Salon, Grocery, Electronics, Service, or Custom). The platform loads only the modules your business needs — no clutter.

2

Set Up Your Company Profile

Go to Settings → Company Settings. Add your TRN number, logo, address, and VAT details. These appear on every FTA-compliant invoice you generate.

3

Add Your Products & Services

Go to Inventory → Add Product. Set name, price, barcode, stock quantity, and low-stock threshold. The system alerts you automatically before you run out.

4

Create Your First Sale

Open the POS screen (shortcut: Alt+P). Scan or click products, select a customer, choose payment method, and click Charge. Receipt is generated instantly with full VAT breakdown.

5

Invite Your Team

Go to Employees → Add Employee. Assign roles (Cashier, Manager, Admin) with RBAC permissions. Each employee gets their own secure login with audit trail.

POS System

Offline First: The POS works 100% without internet. All sales are stored locally and sync automatically when connection is restored — no data loss, ever.
