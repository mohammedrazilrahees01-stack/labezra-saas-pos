Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Labezra ERP platform, including government entities, enterprises, and businesses operating in the United Arab Emirates and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Please read this policy carefully before using our services.

1. Introduction

Labezra ERP ("Labezra", "we", "us", or "our") is a comprehensive enterprise resource planning solution developed specifically for businesses and government agencies in the UAE and KSA. We are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of all personal data processed through our platform.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect personal and organisational data. It also outlines your rights and the legal frameworks governing our data processing activities in both jurisdictions. By accessing or using Labezra ERP, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Account Data: Full name, username, email address, phone number, job title, and authentication credentials of system users and administrators.
  • Organisational Data: Business name, Trade License number, Tax Registration Number (TRN), company address, and other business identifiers required for FTA compliance.
  • Transaction Data: Sales invoices, purchase orders, payment records, VAT returns, inventory movements, payroll information, and financial statements generated through the platform.
  • Customer & Vendor Data: Contact information, TRN details, transaction history, and communication records related to customers and suppliers.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device identifier, session data, access timestamps, and activity logs for security and system improvement purposes.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process personal and organisational data for the following lawful purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To operate, maintain, and improve the Labezra ERP platform and its features, including billing, reporting, and system administration.
  • Regulatory Compliance: To fulfil obligations under UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) requirements, including the generation of compliant VAT invoices, e-invoicing (Fatoorah), and financial audit trails.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To monitor system access, detect anomalies, prevent unauthorised use, and maintain the integrity of the platform.
  • Support & Communication: To respond to technical support requests, send system notifications, and communicate important updates regarding the platform.
  • System Improvement: To analyse aggregated usage patterns and improve functionality, performance, and the overall user experience.

4. UAE Data Protection Compliance

Labezra ERP operates in full compliance with the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL), which entered into force on 2 January 2022. This law establishes the rights of data subjects and obligations of data controllers and processors operating within the UAE.

Specifically, Labezra adheres to the following principles mandated by the UAE PDPL:

  • Lawfulness, fairness, and transparency in all data processing activities
  • Purpose limitation — data is collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes
  • Data minimisation — only data necessary for the stated purpose is collected
  • Accuracy — reasonable steps are taken to ensure data is kept accurate and up-to-date
  • Storage limitation — data is retained only as long as necessary for legal and operational requirements
  • Integrity and confidentiality — appropriate technical and organisational measures are in place

Additionally, Labezra ERP maintains compliance with the UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) requirements for electronic tax invoicing, VAT record-keeping, and financial reporting under the UAE VAT Law (Cabinet Decision No. 52 of 2017).

5. Saudi Arabia Data Protection Compliance

For operations in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Labezra ERP complies with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), issued under Royal Decree M/19 dated 9/2/1443H (corresponding to September 2021), and its implementing regulations published by the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA).

In accordance with the Saudi PDPL, we ensure that:

  • Personal data of Saudi residents is collected and processed only on lawful grounds, including explicit consent where required
  • Cross-border data transfers outside Saudi Arabia are subject to appropriate safeguards and are conducted only where permitted under the PDPL
  • Data subjects are informed of their rights and can exercise them through our designated privacy channels
  • Sensitive personal data (including financial, health-related, and identification data) is afforded enhanced protection
  • Data breaches are reported to SDAIA and affected individuals within the timeframes mandated by law

Labezra also complies with the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) e-invoicing (Fatoorah) requirements applicable to businesses operating in Saudi Arabia.

6. Data Security

We employ industry-standard technical and organisational security measures to protect personal and business data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction:

  • Encryption: All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.2 or higher. Sensitive data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.
  • Access Controls: Role-based access control (RBAC) ensures that users can only access data relevant to their authorised functions.
  • Audit Logs: All system access and data modifications are logged and retained for security auditing and compliance purposes.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Available for all user accounts to strengthen identity verification.
  • Regular Security Reviews: We conduct periodic vulnerability assessments and penetration testing to identify and remediate security risks.
  • Data Backups: Automated encrypted backups are performed daily with secure off-site storage and tested restoration procedures.

7. Data Retention

Labezra retains personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law:

  • Financial Records: All financial transactions, invoices, VAT records, and related documentation are retained for a minimum of 5 (five) years in compliance with UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2015 (Commercial Companies Law) and ZATCA requirements in Saudi Arabia.
  • Account Data: User account data is retained for the duration of the subscription and for 12 months following account termination, unless a shorter period is required by law.
  • System Logs: Security and access logs are retained for 12 months for audit and incident investigation purposes.
  • Backup Data: Backup copies are retained for 90 days on a rolling basis before secure deletion.

Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, personal data is securely deleted or anonymised in accordance with best practices and regulatory requirements.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the following rights in respect of your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You may request that inaccurate or incomplete personal data be corrected.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal retention obligations.
  • Right to Restriction: You may request that we restrict the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: Where technically feasible, you may request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You may object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing or other legitimate interest-based processing.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of the above rights, please submit a written request to our Privacy Team using the contact details provided below. We will respond within 30 days of receipt of your request, or within any shorter period required by applicable law.
